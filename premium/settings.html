Make evening changes automatic

Start with original colors. Choose a comfortable evening appearance; you can turn it off any time.

  1. Choose sunset timing with a saved location, or your own evening and morning times.
  2. Set warmth and optional dimming. Preview is temporary and restores automatically after 20 seconds.
  3. Save and enable Smart. Wait for “Settings saved”; saving is separate from Windows accepting the display adjustment.

Prefer Manual? Skip setup, then adjust the warmth or dimming controls when you are ready. Nothing is enabled by skipping.

Quick guide & safety

Pin it for quicker access. Open Night Light, right-click its icon on the Windows taskbar, then choose Pin to taskbar. Windows controls whether this option is available. The small moon near the clock is a separate background-app icon and may be under the hidden-icons arrow.

Your choices, not inferred sleep. Smart Comfort uses your chosen schedule. “Evening ready” is when you want your target appearance, not a measured bedtime. A VPN does not change your saved coordinates; update them yourself when you move.

Keep it handy. Right-click Night Light in Start → More → Pin to taskbar. Right-click the tray moon near your clock to open this panel.

Warmth & dimming. Warmth reduces blue output. Dimming lowers software output, not your monitor’s hardware brightness.

Smart. Follows local sunset and sunrise. Adjusting a slider holds your choice for one hour. Pause restores original colors; Resume rejoins the schedule gently.

Manual. Keeps your own settings. App Off stops automation until you enable it again.

Hold to compare. Temporarily restores your actual display. Release, leave the window, or wait 10 seconds to restore the filter.

Emergency reset. Ctrl+Alt+Shift+N restores original colors and stops Smart while the app is running.

Windows Night Light on or unknown pauses this filter to avoid stacking. This app does not measure sleep or melatonin and is not medical treatment.

Display conflict. If the software transform changes outside Night Light, it stops writing. Check Windows and close any other color tool before retrying. Readback checks Windows software state; it does not identify which app changed it, measure your physical screen or detect every color pipeline.
