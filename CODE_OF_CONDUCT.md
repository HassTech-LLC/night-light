# Community Conduct

Night Light by HT welcomes respectful, constructive participation.

- Treat contributors and users with respect regardless of background or experience.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Do not harass, threaten, dox, or publish private information.
- Keep health claims accurate and correct misinformation without shaming users.
- Maintainers may edit or remove abusive, unsafe, or off-topic contributions and may restrict participation when needed.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project elsewhere.
